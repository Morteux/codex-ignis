      Planet modifiers

      Each planet has a small chance of containing a modifier that will affect the colony should the world be colonized. They may be positive, negative or mixed, identified by the color of the modifier's border.

      Random anomalies can generate a great variety of planet modifiers but those usually do not have any effects.

      Terraforming candidates[editar | editar código]

      Terraforming Candidates are modifiers that have a small chance to appear on uninhabitable planets and are always added to worlds ruined by a crisis. The modifiers allow the planet to be terraformed if the Climate Restoration technology has been researched. They do not appear if the required expansion is not installed. The chance for a planet to have the modifier is scaled by the Habitable Worlds game setting.

      Modifier Planet Chance Required perk DLC
      Terraforming Candidate
      Though barren and devoid of life, this planet has water in frozen form and may once have been home to a thriving ecosystem. With the right technology, it could be terraformed into a habitable world.
      		 Barren 1% —
      Frozen Terraforming Candidate
      Though cold to the point of being unable to sustain life, the frozen water on this world could allow us to terraform it into a habitable world, if we can find the right method.
      		 Frozen 1% Hydrocentric
      Toxic Terraforming Candidate
      Though the noxious gases on this world make it unfit for life, with enough effort they can be broken down into less lethal compounds, eventually allowing colonization.
      		 Toxic 15% Detox
