      Decisions represent colony-wide undertakings. Most decisions have a cost to be used, either when taking the decision or when agreeing to the deal that made the decision available in the first place. Some decisions also take some time to implement, from a few days up to 10 years; these decisions go into the colony's construction queue, and can be paused and resumed by reordering, like other constructions.

      Specialized colony decisions[editar | editar código]

      Specialized colony decisions cost 1000 Unity and take half a year to implement and will give a colony a permanent modifier that will change the colony to a specialized designation, block the construction of certain buildings, change the types of districts that can be constructed on the planet, and create unique jobs, most of which provide empire-wide bonuses. Abolishing a specialization costs 2500 Unity. Gestalt Consciousness cannot use specialized designations.

      An empire can only have one of each specialized designation modifier with the exception of Thrall-World, which can be used on multiple colonies.

      Ring Worlds, Habitats and Nanite Worlds and arkships cannot be specialized. An Ecumenopolis can only be specialized as a Penal Colony.

      Consecrate World[editar | editar código]

      The Consecrate World decision requires the Consecrated Worlds ascension perk and can be used on up to three uncolonized celestial objects inside borders at the same time to give the empire a positive modifier but if the celestial object is habitable it prevents it from being colonized or terraformed. Both the decision cost and the modifier effects scale with the number of planets consecrated. Worlds can be unconsecrated for the same cost it took to consecrate them. Consecrated worlds have a golden aura around them in the system map. Consecrating a Holy World gives a cumulative +30 Opinion with the Holy Guardians but deconsecrating a Holy World removes all opinion bonuses.

      Consecrated worlds Cost Amenities Unity Spiritualist Ethics Attraction
      1 500 +5% +10% +10%
      2 1000 +10% +20% +20%
      3 1500 +20% +40% +40%

      If a system with a consecrated world is lost the empire will gain −5% Happiness for 10 years.
