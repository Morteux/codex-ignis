      Empire modifiers

      De Stellaris Wiki
      Ir a la navegación Ir a la búsqueda

      Version

      This article has been verified for the current PC version (4.5) of the game.

      Empire modifiers are bonuses or maluses gained through a variety of actions such as anomaly or event choices. They affect the entire empire and as such have a large impact on the game.
