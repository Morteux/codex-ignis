Design rules and decisions of the planet simulator. Pending work lives in `tasks.md`; the full history of finished tasks is in git (GDD before 2026-11-19).

All current data is tagged 4.5.2 and stays `unverified` until P14 (some values come from 4.3/4.4 pages, e.g. base job outputs).
- **Scope:** only the simulator. Wiki, bingo, legal pages and tech tree (`TECH_TREE_GDD.md`) are out.
- **Rule #1:** never invent rules, numbers or names. Missing or ambiguous wiki data → ask. Every simplification is documented in code and in §8.
- **Wiki requirement columns:** a red-cross icon (`No.png`) before a requirement means "not" (not available on / not with). Markdown generated before 2026-11-19 drops the icon (read those requirement columns from the HTML); the parser now keeps every image as `![label](file.ext)`, e.g. `![No](24px-No.png)`.

## 1. Goal
Browser sandbox to theorycraft the economy of **one planet**: districts, buildings, empire context → monthly production, upkeep and net per resource. Not a galaxy or pop simulator.

## 2. Principles
- **Data-driven & versioned:** gameplay data in data files; provenance per collection (`DATA_COLLECTIONS` in `data-version.js`, exceptions in `PROVENANCE_OVERRIDES`): `rulesetId`, source, `verification` (`unverified` | `wiki-verified` | `game-data-verified` | `approximated` | `unsupported`).
- **Full staffing:** every job created is staffed; one pop per job. The per-job slider is only an optional override. Extra pops come only from the manual Civilians input.
- **Inspectable and honest:** every number explains its source; manual/estimated values are labelled; unmodelled mechanics are listed, never faked.
- **Deterministic**, no backend, localStorage only.
- **Legality enforced:** illegal combinations are never allowed (no sandbox mode).
- **Unmodelled requirements are shown, never hidden** (owner rule 2026-11-19): a requirement the simulator does not model and has no plan to model (origin, planetary feature, planet modifier, relic attunement...) never blocks the option, but is listed next to it as "Requirement not modelled: ..." (`{ type: "unmodelled", i18nKey }` in `requires`).
- **Unlock assumptions:** all technologies researched; no tradition unlocked (exception: tradition-gated policy options and edicts are available); DLC requirements ignored.
- **Language:** es/en/jp UI. Until P15 no new es/jp translations: every new text is English (from the wiki) in all three languages. Key parity between languages is mandatory.

## 3. Architecture
- Vanilla JS ES modules, no build. Shared state `js/planet-state.js` (body, designation, ethics, authority, civics, species type, policies, planet name/id, empire id/modified, `rulesetId`).
- Simulator modules (P23.3, one responsibility each): `planet-sim.js` (page entry: `refresh()` and start-up; re-exports `showNotice`, `capturePlanetConfig`, `applyPlanetConfig`), `planet-sim-state.js` (state of the edited planet; the values several modules reassign live in the `sim` object, and it registers the job swaps before any other module reads `JOBS`), `planet-sim-rules.js` (capital sets, district definitions per body, slots, caps, deposit features, blockers, planet modifiers, requirements), `planet-sim-economy.js` (economy engine: `computeTotals()`, staffing), `planet-sim-text.js` (names, icons, number formats, requirement and job swap texts), `sim-text-helpers.js` (signed numbers and percentages, resource and job names; also used by the Edicts and Sucursales tabs and `job-swaps.js`), `planet-sim-dom.js` (shared page elements, notice banner), `planet-sim-tabs.js` (Buildings/Districts and main tab bars), one view per tab or panel (`planet-sim-planet-ui.js` designation, capital, size and slot board; `planet-sim-catalog-ui.js` buildings and districts catalogs; `planet-sim-jobs-ui.js` job rows; `planet-sim-production-ui.js` stats, summary, production, timeline, comparison; `planet-sim-conditions-ui.js` modifiers and conditions; `planet-sim-body-tab.js` deposit features, blockers, planet modifiers; `planet-sim-branch-tab.js`), `planet-sim-config.js` (capture/apply a saved planet) and `planet-sim-events.js` (language change, empire switched, policies reset, edicts removed, shared state changes), `planet-sim-deeplink.js` (T3405: `planet-sim.html#building=<id>` / `#district=<id>` open and highlight that catalogue entry; capitals highlight the capital control, branch office buildings open their tab; entries not shown with the current empire or body, and unknown ids, are explained in the notice). Other modules: `empire.js` (Imperio tab, policies), `empire-profiles.js`, `celestial.js`, `planet-summary.js`, `planet-library.js` / `planet-library-ui.js`, `confirm-dialog.js`, `info-panels.js`, `stacking-rules.js`, `data-version.js`. Data: `planet-data.js`, `body-type-data.js`, `empire-data.js`, `civic-rules.js`, `designation-data.js`, `celestial-data.js`, `building-requirements.js`, `policy-data.js`, `edict-data.js`, `branch-office-data.js`. The building catalogue shows "Researched with: <technology>" links to the tech tree (new tab) from `tech-unlock-links.js`, generated from the tech tree data (`tools/build-tech-links.mjs`, `TECH_TREE_GDD.md` §6); they are information only and do not change the research assumption of §2.
- Images from `STELLARIS_ASSETS_BASE` (`asset-config.js`); UI icons in `img/ui/`.
- Conventions: building/job effects stored ×100 as on the wiki, `divideEffect()` once when computing; jobs, pops, housing and amenities are **displayed ×100** (100 = 1 pop) and calculated in real units. CSS: never set `display` on an element toggled with `hidden` without `:not([hidden])`.
- Test: `tests/policies-smoke.py` (Playwright, English UI, run from the project root).

## 4. Planet model
- **Slots:** 6 base (slot 1 = capital) +3 per district specialization, cap 21. Stable slot keys.
- **Capital:** tiers I–V for 3 families (standard, hive, machine); tier kept when the authority changes. Habitats use their own capitals for every empire (P10.4): Habitat Administration / Central Control / System Control, tiers II–IV, jobs by authority, +300 amenities if gestalt; additional jobs by civic/species, Worker Cooperative Stewards and max districts per system body are not modelled. Switching body keeps the closest tier not above the current one (tier I → II on a habitat).
- **Districts:** urban (2 specialization slots, min 1 copy), generator/mining/agriculture (1 slot each). The four groups keep their keys on every body; their definitions come from the body type (§4.1): on an Ecumenopolis the primary is the Residential Arcology (+1500 housing, +300 Clerks or gestalt +900 Logistic Drones, +300 with Anti-Gravity Engineering) and the three secondary groups are Unspecialized Arcology sets (+1500 housing, one urban specialization each, its housing loss ×3). Specialization jobs ×3 per copy on an Ecumenopolis. Ring World: City / Hive / Nexus Segment primary by authority (+2500/+3000/+2500 housing; +300 Clerks +200 Enforcers / +1500 Logistic Drones +200 Synapse Drones / +1000 Logistic Drones +200 Tech-Drones; Anti-Gravity Engineering +500 Logistic Drones on Hive/Nexus) and three Unspecialized Segment sets (+2500 housing) taking urban specializations, Energy Generation or Agricultural Focus (−200 housing like the urban ones); specialization jobs and the secondary housing loss ×5. Habitat (P10.4): Habitation District primary (+1000 housing, no jobs, urban specializations except the research ones) and the three secondary groups hold the Reactor District (generator specializations), Astro-Mining Bay (mining specializations) and Research District (no specializations, unlocks no building slots), each +300 housing; each habitat resource district has a manual cap (orbital deposits: 1 + half of the deposit size), optional field `districts.<key>.cap` saved with the planet, empty = no limit; districts above the cap stay built (+ disabled). The second primary specialization needs Habitat Expansion (researched). Void Dwellers / Voidborne ×1.5 not modelled. Size (P10.7): the wiki gives a size only for habitable megastructures; converted worlds (Ecumenopolis, Hive / Machine / Nanite World) keep the planet's size. The size input is the number of district slots, free (1–50) because features, blockers, planet modifiers, traditions and civics (e.g. Planet Forgers +3 on Volcanic worlds) change it; its tooltip lists the wiki ranges (planets 12–25, moons 10–15, homeworlds 18–21). Megastructures and habitats have a fixed size (Ring World 10, Shattered Ring World 25, Habitat 6): the size input is disabled and the user's size comes back on other bodies; districts above the size stay built (the + button is disabled). The Shattered Ring World keeps the regular districts (its resource districts are uncapped; deposit caps are not modelled anyway). Switching body type resets the specializations the new districts do not offer (notice); their buildings turn inactive. Urban specializations: Mixed/Heavy/Civilian Industry, Military Defenses, Commercial Nexus, Research Enclave and the Engineering/Physics/Society research specializations (not on a Habitat primary district), Administrative Hub (+100 Administrators: Bureaucrats, Hive Mind Synapse Drones, Machine Intelligence Coordinators; not Spiritualist / Rogue Servitor) and Spiritual Enclave (+100 Priests, Spiritualist) (P10.9). Primary-only (not in secondary urban districts): Urban Expansion (+200 housing, no jobs, its slots permit every building set except Generator / Mining / Farming), Archives (+20 of each researcher, +40 Administrators; with Rogue Servitor no Administrators and the +200 Bio-Trophies are shown as not modelled) and the Relic Central Spire. Sanctuary Enclave (Rogue Servitor, Bio-Trophies) is not modelled. Total copies ≤ planet size (editable, default 20). Housing: urban +1000 (hive +1200, +600 more on Hive World; machine +1000), −200 per chosen urban specialization, Agrarian Idyll −200; resource districts +200 (gestalt +100, Agrarian Idyll +300). Technology housing bonuses apply per copy (listed in the housing tooltip of the header): Weather Control Systems +100 urban; Anti-Gravity Engineering +200 city, +100 nexus/hive cluster (not with Agrarian Idyll, which cannot research it); Agrarian Utopias +200 agriculture (Agrarian Idyll only). Tradition ones (Public Works Division) do not.
- **District body types:** a district or building with no body-type requirement on the wiki is available on every body type.
- **Inactive, never deleted:** a building whose slot is locked, whose requirements are lost or whose slot specialization no longer permits its set stays built but inactive (no jobs/resources), drawn with `img/ui/blocker_frame_yellow.png`, tooltip with the reason; demolishing is always manual. Applies to empire switches and planet loads (report how many became inactive).
- **Invalid choices reset with a notice:** designation → "no designation"; district specialization removed (its buildings turn inactive); policy option → group default.
- **Jobs UI:** "Jobs created / Jobs assumed staffed"; click ±10, Ctrl/Cmd ±100, Shift = 100 %/0 %; no reset button; the notice under the summary explains only the click controls.

### 4.2 Planetary features, blockers and planet modifiers (P10.8, wiki 4.5.2 *Planetary features*, *Planet modifiers*)
**Owner decisions (2026-11-20):**
- Resource district limit is active, worked the other way round: the user does not pick the planet's features; the simulator computes the fewest deposit features the built resource districts need on this planet type (Cuerpo celeste tab, "Required deposit features"; data in `js/planetary-features-data.js`). Warning when that number is below 4 or above 15 (the wiki's range, which depends on planet size; no formula is applied).
- Strategic resource deposits (needed by the strategic specializations): the simulator picks the worst variant by default (e.g. Crystalline Caverns +0.05 rather than Crystal Forest +0.10) and the user can choose another one.
- Selectable blockers (only useful ones): Collapsed Spire (Relic), the five Blazing Scourge blockers, Nanite Harvest Basin, Shroudstone and Ruined Sanctum of the Dreamer. Blockers that only remove districts are covered by the manual size.
- Planet modifiers: a selectable list with direct effects (resources, but also stability, crime, amenities, happiness and other planet values), not only resources.

**Deposit features (P10.8, part 1):** applies to the 9 regular planets and Volcanic, Gaia, Tomb and Relic Worlds (artificial bodies and converted worlds have none). Exact search: fewest features, then least spare capacity; features with the same caps are interchangeable (the first in wiki order is shown, the others as alternatives). Sulfuric Lava (Volcanic) counts for both Magma Conduits and Thermotechnic Forums. A district group with no feature on the planet type cannot be built (+ disabled, note): Relic Worlds have no agriculture feature. Uncapped: agriculture on an Ocean World with Anglers / Trawling Operations (Marine Machines / Maritime Robotics share the keys); Shattered Ring World and habitats keep their own rules. Not modelled: district-cap buildings (Industrial Mining Site...), Subterranean origin mining, features from events, the Alien Zoo feature.

**Requirement audit (P10.10):** requirement columns re-read from the HTML wiki pages (the regenerated markdown drops the red cross "not" and the Agriculture specializations table). Fixes: Experimentation Chambers / Complex / Nexus need the Experimental Sentencing family (Experimental Sentencing, Judicial R&D, Corrective Consciousness), not Materialist; Uplink Node / Network Junction / System Conflux not with Rogue Servitor. Shown as unmodelled requirements (never block): not Wilderness (Agricultural Focus, Stronghold / Fortress, Synaptic Nodes line), not the Command Center capital (Commercial Zones, Holo-Theatres), Empire uses food (Hydroponics Farms), Astral Siphon astral action, Caravaneer deal (Waste Reprocessing Center). Technology requirements stay ignored.

**Anglers on wet-climate worlds (P10.10):** Anglers-family civics (Anglers, Trawling Operations, Marine Machines, Maritime Robotics) on an Ocean, Continental or Tropical world block Agricultural Focus and Agrarian Villages (rule `notAll`) and unlock Aquaculture (+50 Anglers, +50 Pearl Divers) and Coastal Hamlets. Planet-wide swaps (Jobs page): every Farmer becomes an Angler (+6 food, +2 trade) and every Artisan a Pearl Diver (+6 consumer goods, +2 trade; upkeep −4 minerals, −3 food). Angler swap weight 50 is above the Gas Extractor's 20, so with Exotic Gas Capture the Farmer jobs stay Anglers, with −3 food +0.5 exotic gases each. As with the other swaps, effects keyed on the base job (Artisan efficiency, building job modifiers) do not apply to the swapped job.

**Strategic deposits (P10.8, part 2):** Volatile Motes Harvesting (generator), Rare Crystal Extraction and Betharian Processing (mining), Exotic Gas Capture (agriculture) need a strategic deposit (`STRATEGIC_DEPOSITS`, requirement `deposit`: only on bodies with deposit features whose type can host one of its deposits). The deposit is added automatically, the worst variant by default, selectable in the Cuerpo celeste tab and saved with the planet (optional field `strategicDeposits` {specialization: deposit}); its caps count first in the required features. Deposit bonuses "+X per 100 <category>" = +X per staffed job of the Technicians / Miners / Farmers category (`JOB_CATEGORIES`, swaps included), per-job bonus with the workforce bonuses breakdown line. Job swaps ("zone on planet"): every Technician / Miner / Farmer of the planet becomes Mote Harvester / Crystal Miner / Gas Extractor. Betharian Processing: −2 minerals +6 energy per staffed Miners-category job, once for the planet. Fumaroles and Stifling Atmosphere are Volcanic only; the other deposits can be on any natural non-volcanic planet (mining deposits cannot appear on Volcanic Worlds). Gas Extractor output from the Jobs page (+0.5 exotic gases; the specialization page says +0.75: P14). Owner-confirmed 2026-11-20: the per-category reading (swapped jobs count) and the Gas Extractor value.

**Blockers (P10.8, part 3):** `BLOCKERS` in `js/planetary-features-data.js`, Cuerpo celeste tab checklist, saved with the planet (optional field `blockerIds`). A selected blocker whose body or requirement no longer fits stays selected with no effect (reason shown). Collapsed Spire (Relic): locks the first primary specialization slot and blocks the Central Spire while present. Blazing Scourge blockers (Fire Cult civic; not Gaia, artificial or converted worlds): flat output (+5 energy / unity / minerals, step 5, breakdown line "Blockers") or per staffed Miner / Technician (+0.25 exotic gases / volatile motes) and +500 amenities each; −20 % habitability not modelled. Nanite Harvest Basin (+0.5 nanites), Shroudstone and Ruined Sanctum of the Dreamer (+1 Zro): origin shown as unmodelled requirement; Shroudstone's Covenant bonus not modelled. New resources: Nanites, Zro.

**Planet modifiers (P10.8, part 4):** `js/planet-modifier-data.js` (142 modifiers generated from the wiki tables, every one with at least one modelled effect), Cuerpo celeste tab grouped like the wiki, saved with the planet (optional field `planetModifierIds`). Modelled effects: % resources from jobs (own breakdown line), per-job bonuses by job category, happiness / stability / crime points (conditions rows "Planet modifiers"; happiness and "if Individualist" only outside gestalt), flat and % housing / amenities (before the conditions), max districts per group (change the required deposit features), worker / specialist output and job efficiency, upkeep from jobs, pop housing usage. Everything else is listed as "Not modelled" with the wiki text, and the source (event, origin, unique world) is shown as information. Owner rule 2026-11-20: every text shown must be in the selected language; these data strings move to i18n keys in P13.8. Spawn weight ×0 on a planet type hides the modifier there; "×0 if <other modifier>" makes them exclusive (the first one in wiki order wins). Not included: orbital deposit modifiers, terraforming candidates, Doomsday / Red Giant stages, Arkship, Grand refurbishment and Shroud-Forged modifiers. Blazing Scourge blocker amenities are now counted before the conditions (fix).

## 5. Empire
- Ethics wheel (3 points, gestalt exclusive), 7 authorities, civics (max 3) with requirement/exclusion rules; civic keys derive from the icon file name (`principios_del_juego.md`), English name = wiki name.
- **Origin** (P10.6, game files 4.5.4): an "Origin" panel lists the modelled origins, only **Wilderness** (none = no modelled origin, `originId` null, saved with the empire). Wilderness needs the Hive Mind authority and none of its 13 excluded hive civics (they are not offered while it is chosen); leaving Hive Mind drops it. The authority stays Hive Mind and is shown as the **Mutualistic Intelligence** (its effects replace the Hive Mind ones, all informational). On bodies with the standard district set (regular planets, Gaia, Tomb, Relic) its districts replace the regular ones: Communal Biome (primary, +100 housing, −2 energy, 2 slots for the Wilderness urban specializations Heartgrove, Moltgut, Thinkstem, Singing Cliffs, Harmonic Core, Threadway, Command Bulb), Photosynthesis Field / Hollow Mountain / Orchard Forest (+100 housing, +200 Technician / Miner / Farmer, −1 energy / −1 food / −1 energy; specializations Solar Mycolungs / Orespine / Bloomloom plus the strategic ones); Wilderness specializations do not change housing. Capitals Planetary Tendrils, Root Bundle, Root Complex, Root Nexus, Imperial Complex (tiers I–V, food upkeep). **Biomass** is a resource shown only in costs (district and capital tooltips). ## 6. Economy calculation
- **Order** (`CALCULATION_ORDER` in `stacking-rules.js`, texts `modifierOrder*`): 1 job base output/upkeep → 2 per-job building bonuses, then per-job edict outputs/upkeep (own breakdown line), then per-job branch office bonuses (own line) → 3 efficiency (designation + building + stratum + Production Policy + edicts, one pool, floor 0, positive output only) or upkeep change (per job + edicts on all jobs), × staffed jobs → 4 % of job output (buildings, Economic Policy) → 4a stability effect → 4b trade conversion → 5 flat outputs (buildings, designation) and upkeep (capital, districts, buildings) → 6 ethic/authority/stance/edict multipliers on accumulated positive production → 7 empire flat bonuses (external income).
- **Stacking:** modifiers of the same category **add** (owner decision, kept until P14 verifies against game files).
- **Stability effect** (step 4a, category `stabilityEffect`): +0.6 % per point above 50, −1 % per point below (unrounded stability), on the positive output of jobs after steps 1–4 (the step-4 % is not compounded); not on planetary modifiers, and amenities count as a modifier (not affected). It comes before the trade conversion, so converted trade includes it. Conditions are therefore computed before step 4a (designation flat housing/amenities counted in advance). Shown in Conditions, the modifiers panel and the breakdown.
- **Pop upkeep** (P20.3, step 5, categories `popUpkeep` / `popOutput`): every pop pays the species type upkeep (§5.3) and the living standard's consumer goods per stratum (ruler / specialist / worker jobs and civilians; special-stratum jobs such as Colonists and Neural Chips have no stratum and pay only the species upkeep). Added like the other upkeep, untouched by job upkeep modifiers; own breakdown lines "Pop upkeep: species type" and "Pop upkeep: living standard". The living standard's trade per pop and civilian output are pop output: they join job output (stability effect, trade conversion, empire multipliers), line "Pops: living standard". Its flat happiness goes to the conditions (not in gestalt); researcher efficiency (Academic Privilege) and stratum efficiency go to the usual job efficiency categories. Edicts change pop upkeep per resource (P20.4, category `popUpkeepMult`, added, floor 0, line "Pop upkeep: edicts"), never job upkeep. Not modelled: the "no deficit" conditions (always assumed met), political power, per-stratum happiness, Phototrophic half upkeep.
- **Trade conversion:** trade from jobs and civilians, after job efficiency and before empire %; trade kept = trade × rate, the rest becomes the policy's resources (breakdown lines per resource). Branch-office trade will join it. Monthly Trades excluded.
- **External income:** empire flat bonuses (influence) are shown apart from planet net, with a "total with external income" line.
- **Consistency:** produced + consumed = sum of breakdown lines = net (tolerance 0.001), otherwise a bug warning.
- **Panels:** per-resource breakdown (click a Net row), active-modifiers panel (source, target, value, category), "i" explanation panels (`js/info-panels.js`: static `.info-btn` + `createInfoPanel`, also on the Imperio tab; open state kept across re-renders/language; texts use the current numbers where they apply, e.g. the trade conversion of the Production panel).
- **Scenario A/B comparison (P11.1, `js/scenario-compare.js`):** "Pin the current planet as scenario A" takes a snapshot of the planet's net resources and conditions (stability, happiness, crime, free housing, pops); scenario B is always the live planet. Each row shows A, B and the change B − A; changed rows come first (largest change first), green when better, red when worse (more crime is worse); not applicable values show "—". Planet net only (no external income). The snapshot is kept in memory only (not saved, not exported).
- **Monthly stockpile timeline (P11.2, `js/stockpile-timeline.js`):** per stockpiled resource the planet touches, a starting stockpile input (default 0, memory only) and the stockpile after 1 / 12 / 60 / 120 months = start + planet net × months, floored at 0. The planet stays as it is (no growth, construction or events); storage caps and external income are not included. Research (not a stockpile), amenities (planet value) and trade (converted by the trade policy) are left out.
- **Deficit and exhaustion flags (P11.3):** a timeline resource with a negative net is a deficit: its Flags cell reads "deficit: runs out in N months" (N = ceil(start / −net), the first whole month after which the stockpile is 0) or "deficit: stockpile empty" when it starts at 0; horizons at or past N are marked red, and an alert above the table lists every deficit, soonest first. The game's deficit penalties are not modelled (warning only).

### 6.1 Job swaps audit (P21.1, wiki 4.5.2 *Jobs*)
Data in `js/job-swap-data.js` (`jobSwaps` collection): 89 swaps (every row whose source reads "<Base> job swap"), with base job, weight, empire type, output per job (upkeep included, amenities ÷100, untyped "+1 Research" = +1 of each type), non-resource effects, conditions and the stacked output changes.
- **Rule (wiki):** a swap replaces every job of its base job on the planet; it inherits the base job's modifiers (building bonuses, efficiency, edicts, % from jobs); with several met swaps the highest weight wins. "Save exceptions, the output changes stack": only the changes the wiki lists on the winning job ("With X swap conditions: …", `withSwaps`) are added (assumption, §9). Swaps whose conditions reference other swaps ("Noble conditions AND Science Director conditions") use `{ type: "swap" }`.
- **Conditions:** civics, ethics, authority, origin (Wilderness, P10.6), designation, body type, modelled buildings and specializations, planet modifiers and branch office buildings are evaluated. Other origins, cybernetic / Shroud authorities, habitability, unmodelled buildings (Galactic Stock Exchange, Citadel of Faith, Holo-Museum, Numistic Shrine, Ministry of Culture, Astrometeorology Observatory, Ranger Lodge, Faculty of Archaeostudies, Nanite Research Facility, Genomic Research Facility...), zones (Memory Hall, Xenobiological Zoo, Subterranean Contact, Portal Research Area) and features (Crashed Ships, Cave Shroom Veins, Metallic Puddles...) are `unmodelled`: for swaps they are **never met** (the swap is listed, not applied), unlike building requirements where they never block. Federation perks (A Union of Faith) are never met; traditions (Nanotech) are met.
- **Base jobs not in the simulator** (Telepath, Evaluator, Lubrication Terminal; T1011: Transference Drone): their swaps are info only. T1011 adds the Spawning Drone (Cradle of Rebirth), so its swaps are modelled (Offspring / Genomic Drone keep their unmodelled conditions). Gestalt upkeep variants are `upkeepByType`.
- **Gaps on the wiki:** no weight for the event jobs (Subterranean, Dimensional Portal, Space-Time Anomaly, Vent Researcher, Munitions Decommissioner, Dismantler, Cave Cleaner, Nemma Miner, Titan Hunter, Puddle Technician) nor for Devoted Adept (`weight: null`; P21.2 decides their precedence). Pyromancer lists the Death Chronicler block twice (counted once). Numistic Priest: +6 trade output and −2 trade upkeep, stored as net +4. "A; B" in the conditions is read as AND except where the items cannot hold together (Scrap Miner, Culture Worker, Zookeeper, Science Director, Merchant: one of).
- **Already modelled before P21 (hard-coded):** Thermoforger (Volcanic), Crystal Miner / Mote Harvester / Gas Extractor (zones), Angler / Pearl Diver (Anglers + wet climate); Priest, Death Priest and Educator are created directly by buildings and specializations. P21.2 replaces these with the generic step.
- **Engine (P21.2, `js/job-swaps.js`):** every job source (capital, districts, specializations, buildings, branch office) adds its base jobs; once all are known the swaps are resolved with the active buildings, chosen specializations, planet modifiers and branch office buildings, and every job of a base job becomes the winning swap. Weight `null` counts as 0; ties keep data order. Empire type: the wiki Empire column, except the Angler ("any": its condition lists Marine Machines / Maritime Robotics). A swapped job inherits its base job's building bonuses, job efficiency, upkeep changes, edict and branch office per-job bonuses and job categories ("per 100 Miners" counts the Miner swaps). Stacked changes (`withSwaps`, and the Angler under Exotic Gas Capture) are part of the job's own output, before building bonuses: own breakdown line "Jobs: other met job swaps (stacked)" and modifier source "<job> swap conditions"; stacked stability/crime join the job effects. Gestalt upkeep of a swap: its `upkeepByType` variant, otherwise the base job's. Jobs created directly as a swapped job (Temple Priests, State Academy Educators, zone Gas Extractors...) stay as created; their `withSwaps` changes still apply. Civilian swaps (Hedonist, Tourist) are not applied: civilians are a manual count, not jobs. The previous hard-coded swaps (Volcanic, zones, Anglers) are now data.
- **UI (P21.4):** a swapped job row shows "Replaces <base> · weight · conditions" (of a "one of" condition only the met alternatives) and "Also stacks: <job> swap conditions (+X …)". Below the strata, a collapsible "Job swaps not applied (condition not modelled)" lists the swaps of the planet's base jobs that fit the empire and fail only on unmodelled conditions (origin, unmodelled building, habitability…), with those conditions. Wiki names of unmodelled conditions stay English (P13.8).

### 6.2 Total build time of the planet (T2601, game files 4.5.4)
- **Data:** `js/build-time-data.js`, generated by `python tools/extract/build_build_times.py` from the extractor data (`buildings.json`, `districts.json`, `zones.json`, plus `civics.json`, `origins.json`, `designations.json` for the build speed sources). Times are `base_buildtime` in days; a year is 360 days (12 months of 30 days). Buildings and capitals are matched with the T2522 matching (id, English name, icon); districts with a hand-kept table in the script (simulator district → game district by body type, authority and secondary set); specializations by their icon against the zones (every matched zone has 360 days).
- **How the game builds (game files):** each planet has one construction queue for its buildings, districts and zones; its size is the "Build Queue Capacity" (`planet_building_capacity_add`, e.g. Wilderness origin +5, Urban Expansion Frame +1), whose **base value is not in the game files** (engine), so the parallel time is not computed. Build speed: `planet_building_build_speed_mult` ("Infrastructure Build Speed": technologies, Prosperity tradition, edicts, civics Functional Architecture / Constructobot +15 %, Subterranean −10 %, governor traits, many planet and static modifiers…) and one modifier per district type (`planet_district_<type>_build_speed_mult`: Hive World designation +25 % generator / mining / agriculture districts, Wilderness designations +25 % on their district…). How the engine combines the speed with the base time is not in the files.
- **Model:** total = sum, one item after another, of: every capital tier of the set up to the current one (each tier is an upgrade; tier I has no `base_buildtime`, it comes with the colony, and is listed as left out); every active building (the same ones the economy counts), with the earlier tiers of an upgraded building (`upgrades` chain: Alloy Mega-Forge = Foundry 360 + 480); every district copy of the primary and secondary groups; every chosen specialization once. No build speed modifier is applied (the simulator models none); the ones of the current civics, origin and designation are listed in the panel. Left out and said so: items with no game time or no game match (Paradise Dome, habitat tier I), branch office buildings (another empire builds them) and clearing blockers.
- **UI:** fixed planet summary, after the jobs: build time icon (`modifiers/mod_planet_building_time_mult.png`) + "days (years)"; the item opens a panel with the exact years and days, one line per capital / building / district group / specialization, the left-out items, the queue and speed notes, the listed speed sources and the data version.

## 7. Planetary conditions
- Pops = staffed jobs + manual Civilians (Conditions panel, ×100, default 0, no cap, labelled Manual; they consume housing and amenities). Civilian output (wiki 4.5.2 *Jobs* → Special jobs, per pop): Civilian +0.5 amenities, +1 trade; gestalt Maintenance Drone +4 amenities, +1 trade. Their trade gets the stability effect and enters the trade conversion; no stratum efficiency or job % bonuses. Hedonist/Tourist job swaps not modelled.
- Housing usage 1/pop; amenities provided = buildings (rounded) + jobs (rounded down) − 5 lost; usage 1/pop.
- **Edicts** (P18.4): happiness, stability and crime points (category `conditionFlat`) are added before clamping and shown as an "Edicts" row when non-zero (happiness ignored in gestalt; crime only with pops). Per-civilian edict trade (Maintenance Drones) gets the stability effect and the trade conversion like civilian trade.
- **Happiness** (non-gestalt): 50 + amenities effect (bonus min(+20, 20·avail/usage) points, penalty max(−50, 200/3·avail/usage)), clamped 0–100.
- **Stability:** 50 + approval bonus (approval = floor(happiness); +0.6/pt above 50 rounded down, −1/pt below) + stability from jobs − housing penalty 40·missing/pops, clamped 0–100. Job stability (`JOB_EFFECTS`, e.g. Enforcer, Soldier) × staffed jobs is a modifier like amenities: neither the stability effect nor job efficiency changes it. Gestalt: no happiness; amenities effect applies to stability directly.
- **Crime from low happiness** (display only, no invented penalty): wiki 4.5.2 *Colony management* — each pop produces 0 at 100 happiness up to +2 at 0, i.e. 0.02 × (100 − happiness) per pop (the old task text "per point below 50" contradicted the wiki). Shown in Conditions (from pops, job reductions, total ≥ 0) and next to the enforcer/educator crime in the jobs non-resource effects. Gestalt: named Deviancy; with no happiness, deviancy from pops is not modelled (only job reductions). Every value labelled **Manual** (entered by the user), **Estimated** (relies on a simplification: 1 amenity per pop, all pops alike, approval = happiness) or **Derived** (straight from data and wiki rules). Manual extra happiness and stability (default 0, −100…100) for unmodelled sources; they enter happiness/stability before clamping (manual happiness ignored in gestalt) and are saved with the planet.

## 8. Saved data
- localStorage keys `codex-ignis-empires`, `codex-ignis-planets`. JSON export/import (`{type: "planet" | "empire"}`); missing/unsupported `rulesetId` or newer `schemaVersion` → rejected; fields sanitized; new id on import; unknown ids replaced by defaults, never invented.
- Planet: `schemaVersion` (1), `rulesetId`, unique `name`, `celestialTypeId`, `designationId`, `planetSize`, `capitalTier`, districts, `built` (slot key → building id), `staffingOverrides`, `empireId`, `empireSnapshot`, `noEmpire`. Optional fields added later (`manualConditions` {happiness, stability}, `policyIds`, `civilians` (×100 integer), `neuralChips` (×100 integer, Synaptic Lathe), edicts, `branchOffice` {present, illegal, ownerCivicIds ≤3, ownerEmpireId?, ownerEmpireName?, slots ×4} (P19.4; unknown civics/buildings dropped, incompatible or extra civics dropped, illegal without Criminal Heritage → legal; P19.6: `ownerEmpireId` / `ownerEmpireName` only when the owner is a saved megacorporation profile, `ownerCivicIds` being the copy used when that profile is gone)) keep `schemaVersion` 1 if older readers can ignore them.
- Empire: `schemaVersion`, `rulesetId`, `id`, `name`, `ethicIds`, `authorityId`, `originId`, `civicIds`, `speciesType`, `policyIds`, `edictIds`, `livingStandardId` (P20, optional). The planet's `empireSnapshot` carries the same fields.
- Last selected empire: id in `codex-ignis-last-empire`; restored on reload if it still exists (otherwise the default draft). Not exported.
- Deleted empire: the planet keeps the snapshot and shows "no empire". If the empire exists, its current version is used on load. Loading a planet replaces the current one without asking (only unsaved empire changes ask). A file with more districts than size raises the size.

## 9. Known simplifications and assumptions (verify in P14)
- P21.1: job swap output changes stack only as listed on the winning job (`withSwaps`); unmodelled swap conditions are never met.
- Stacking rules are the simulator's own (all additive); parent/sub-category job inheritance not modelled.
- P7.8 data assumptions: Chemical/Synthetic Crystal Plants cost-upkeep-time copied from Exotic Gas Refineries; untyped "Research" = +0.1 of each research type; Waste Reprocessing Center set `origin`, Baol Organic Plant `food`/`factory`; monuments with no upkeep outside Memorialist; settled (not nomadic) variants; Corporate monument icons provisional.
- Production Policy treated as stratum efficiency.
- P10.4: habitat designations "Can build Reactor Districts / Astro-Mining Bays" are locked only when that district's manual cap is 0.
- P7.10: Agrarian Utopias uses the Districts page value (+200); *Society research* says +100. Optimized Nexus and Extended Hives are not on any technology page (they look like gestalt variants of Public Works Division) and are not applied.
- Policy requirement columns partly unreadable on the wiki (tradition/federation conditions as documented).
- Amenity-usage modifiers, crime/deviancy effect on stability, factions, events, traits, origins, leaders: not modelled.

### 9.1 Review against the 4.5.4 game files (P14.4)
Checked against the extractor output of 4.5.4 (`data/generated/stellaris-pc-4.5.4/`, extracted before the installed game moved to 4.6.0). The simulator data is not changed here: the fixes go with the switch to the generated data (T2523, T2528).
- **Confirmed**: Agrarian Utopias gives +200 housing per Agricultural District (`district_farming`, `has_technology = tech_housing_agrarian_idyll` + Agrarian Idyll; +500 on ring world farming segments), so the *Districts* value is right and *Society research* (+100) is wrong. Optimized Nexus and Extended Hives are the gestalt variants of Public Works Division: they are traditions (`tr_prosperity_optimized_nexus`, `tr_prosperity_extended_hives`, +100 housing per Nexus / Hive district, +300 on the Ascended Nexus districts), not technologies, so not applying them is right while traditions are not modelled. City district housing: Weather Control Systems +100, Anti-Gravity Engineering +200 (not with Agrarian Idyll), Agrarian Idyll −200; hive and nexus districts +100 for each technology, as modelled. Habitat Generator, Research and Mining Stations need `district_hab_<type>_max_add > 0`, so locking them when that district's cap is 0 (P10.4) matches the game. Autochthon Monument and Corporate Culture Site have no base upkeep (400 minerals, 360 days).
- **Different in the game**: Chemical Plants and Synthetic Crystal Plants cost 600 minerals (not the 500 copied from Exotic Gas Refineries, which does cost 500); upkeep 3 energy and 480 days are right. Waste Reprocessing Center belongs to the `urban` and `generator` building sets (not `origin`); Baol Organic Plant to `farming` and `factory`. The gestalt Production Policy (`drone_policy`: Extraction Focus / Manufacturing Focus) changes job output (`planet_jobs_simple_drone_produces_mult` / `planet_jobs_complex_drone_produces_mult` ±20 %), not job efficiency (the ethics use `pop_cat_worker_bonus_workforce_mult`), so it belongs to step 4 (% of job output), not step 3.
- **Stacking rules**: the game files only name the modifier kinds (`…_bonus_workforce_mult` = efficiency, `…_produces_mult` = job output %, `…_produces_add`, `…_upkeep_mult`); how the engine adds them is not in the files, so `stackingRules` stays unverified.
- **Not checked**: the untyped "Research" output (+0.1 of each type) and the policy requirements (tradition and federation conditions); they need the switch to the generated data.
